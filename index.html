The Math & Mechanics

How AURELIS Works in 4 Steps

Every transaction you add or import goes through a rigorous four-stage mathematical pipeline.

1

Categorization

Transactions are partitioned into specific categories (Food, Travel, Bills, etc.) to establish localized context.

2

Baseline Computation

For each category, the arithmetic mean (μ) and population standard deviation (σ) are calculated across your history.

3

Z-Score Calculation

Every transaction receives a z-score: z = (amount - μ) / σ, quantifying exact deviation from baseline.

4

Anomaly Flagging

If |z| ≥ threshold (e.g. 3.0σ), the transaction is immediately flagged with severity classification.
