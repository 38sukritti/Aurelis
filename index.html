The Pipeline

Continuous Financial Clarity

Every transaction you record or import effortlessly builds your personal financial intelligence model.

1

Categorization

Transactions are partitioned by purpose (Groceries, Bills, Transport, Dining) to isolate distinct habit profiles.

2

Baseline Learning

The platform computes your authentic rolling mean and standard deviation across your unique spending history.

3

Deviation Detection

New charges are evaluated against their category baseline to measure variance, unexpected fee jumps, and outliers.

4

Actionable Insights

Anomalies are flagged in your executive dashboard with curated tactical masterclasses to help you plug leaks immediately.
